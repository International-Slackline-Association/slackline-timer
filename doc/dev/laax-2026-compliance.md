## 4. Out of scope by design (⚪)

Requirements the software deliberately does not model — the judging/organisation
does them off-app; the console records the outcome:

1. **Per-judge score entry & aggregation** — two-judge averaging (difficulty,
   style), the per-bounce `(1.5^m)−1` difficulty sum, and the proportional-to-40
   normalisation happen on the judges' side; the head judge enters one final
   number per component. Revisit only if the judges ask for in-app judging
   sheets.
2. **Leash-fall counting → penalty** — the 2 pts/leash-fall battle penalty is
   entered as the aggregated `controlPenalty`; timing and scoring stay decoupled
   (a Stop is not auto-interpreted as a penalisable fall — intentional catches
   aren't penalised, and only judges can tell).
3. **Best-trick steal halving** — "landing the opponent's trick halves their
   best-trick score" is judge arithmetic on the entered `bestTrick` value.
4. **The battle changeover ("stabilize + 10 s", auto-resume)** — since ADR 0036
   the software deliberately runs **no battle break clock and no auto-resume**:
   field practice showed the judges pace the whole changeover themselves, so the
   console shows them a control-local pause count-up (`PauseClock`) and the next
   turn starts manually. Modelling line-stabilisation is not automatable; the
   quali advisory break (F2) keeps its configurable count-down.

Purely organisational rules (seeding lists, wild cards, rigging, insurance,
buddy checks, prize money, ethics/appeals, schedules) have no software surface
and are not tracked here.
